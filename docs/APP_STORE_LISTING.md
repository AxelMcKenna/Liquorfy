## Description (4000)

Stop tab-hopping between liquor store websites. Liquorfy compares prices on beer, wine, spirits, RTDs and cider across New Zealand's major liquor and supermarket chains, so you always know where the best deal is.

SEARCH ONCE, COMPARE EVERYWHERE
Search for any drink and see every store's price side by side, with promos and member-only prices clearly marked.

DEALS NEAR YOU
Share your location to see deals at stores within your chosen radius, sorted by distance or discount. Your location stays on your device and is only used to find nearby stores.

PRICES UPDATED DAILY
Prices and promotions are refreshed every day, so the deals you see are current.

FIND REAL VALUE
Sort by price per standard drink or per 100ml to find the best value, not just the lowest sticker price.

PRICE HISTORY AND ALERTS
See how a product's price has moved over time and set an alert to hear when it drops.

WATCHLIST
Save the drinks you buy often and check their prices in one place.

Free to use. No account needed to search and compare.

Liquorfy is for people aged 18 and over. Please drink responsibly. Need support? Visit alcohol.org.nz.

Liquorfy is an independent price comparison service and is not affiliated with any of the retailers listed.

## App Review information

- Sign-in required: No (search and compare work without an account)
- Demo account: create a dedicated email/password account and enter it here. Reviewers use it to test price alerts and account deletion.
- Contact: Axel McKenna, liquorfy@gmail.com

Notes for the reviewer:

> Liquorfy is a price comparison app for alcohol sold by licensed New Zealand retailers. It does not sell alcohol or process payments; "View at Store" opens the retailer's own website. A first-launch screen confirms the user is 18 or over.
>
> Results are location-based within New Zealand. If you are reviewing outside NZ, deny location or use the in-app location picker to choose a NZ location (e.g. Auckland) to see nearby stores. Top deals load without location.
>
> Account deletion: Account → Delete Account.
